# Architecture

## Authority

`Core Laws -> Primary Operator -> Mr. House -> Specialist Agents`

Mr. House is unique and permanent. Hydra cannot target him. He can only improve through bounded self-improvement.

## Hydra

A specialist operational/quality failure retires that active instance and creates two same-role descendants. The descendants inherit the same authority ceiling and constraints, are benchmarked, and only the better compliant candidate becomes active. The other is archived.

Compliance vetoes and Treasury vetoes are successes, not failures.

## Morale

Verified productive behaviour earns persistent Morale Points and ceremonial rewards. Morale cannot change permissions or override vetoes.

## Self-improvement

Agents may improve prompts, workflows, heuristics, tests, task routing and bounded implementation details. Protected components, authority and permissions cannot be altered. Changes must show measurable improvement.
